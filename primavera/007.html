ROLLED MINI FRIES STREET FOOD

With Crunchy Spiced Chickpeas and Yogurt Sauce

Time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Medium (the patience to roll 4 mini omelettes)

Yield: 8 mini rolls (4 portions)

Crispy Chickpeas:

  • 240 g of boiled chickpeas (one can)
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon of cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Yogurt Sauce:

  • 150 g of Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Water or milk to taste(if necessary)

Mini Omelettes:

  • 4 eggs
  • 50 ml of milk
  • 30 g of grated parmesan
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Olive oil for the pan

Assembly:

  • Arugula or purple cabbage for garnish
  • Toothpicks
